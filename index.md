# Tab Compass

A browser extension for people who keep far too many tabs open and lose track of what they were doing. It sorts your
open tabs into topics, saves and snoozes them for later, tidies up by itself if you want, points out duplicates,
manages your tab groups and windows, backs up your open tabs, and tells you where you left off. It works offline;
AI is optional, with your own key or a model on your own computer.

## Get it

- **Firefox**: [addons.mozilla.org](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/tab-compass-organizer/)
- **Chrome** and **Microsoft Edge**: coming soon

## Privacy

Tab Compass has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and its developer never receives any of your data.
Read the [privacy policy](privacy/).
